# Calgary Signs And Shapes

Build a Professional Website for Signs Plus CNC

1. Business Overview

Create a modern, professional, premium website for Signs Plus CNC, a signage, custom printing, and CNC fabrication company based in Calgary, Alberta, Canada.

Our company provides custom signage and fabrication solutions for businesses, contractors, real estate professionals, events, organizations, and individual customers.

The website should position Signs Plus CNC as a reliable, high-quality, professional signage manufacturer capable of producing both individual custom orders and larger commercial projects.

Primary website goals:

Generate customer inquiries and qualified quote requests.

Showcase products, services, and completed work.

Make it easy for customers to upload their designs and request pricing.

Allow customers to purchase selected standard products online.

Build credibility with commercial clients.

Support long-term business growth and search engine visibility.

2. Website Design and Branding

I want a visually impressive, high-end industrial and corporate aesthetic.

The design should feel similar to a premium architectural fabrication or commercial manufacturing company.

Design direction:

Clean, modern, sophisticated layout.

Large, bold typography.

Strong visual hierarchy.

Lots of whitespace.

High-quality images of signage, printing, CNC manufacturing, and completed projects.

Subtle animations and smooth scrolling.

Modern cards with clean borders.

Rounded corners used selectively.

Professional hover effects.

Fully responsive for mobile, tablet, and desktop.

Suggested colour palette:

Charcoal black: #17191C

Crisp white: #FFFFFF

Light grey: #F4F5F6

Industrial grey: #6B7280

Vibrant orange: #F97316 for primary buttons and accents.

The orange should be used sparingly to create strong calls to action. If the company logo uses different brand colours, adapt the website to match the logo.

Typography: Use a modern sans-serif font such as Inter, Manrope, or Plus Jakarta Sans.

Avoid generic templates, excessive gradients, cheap-looking graphics, cluttered sections, and unnecessary animations.

5. Custom Quote Request System

This is one of the most important website features.

Build a professional multi-step quotation form.

Step 1: Project Type

Let the customer select a service category.

Step 2: Project Details

Fields should include:

Product or service type

Width

Height

Measurement unit (inches, feet, or millimetres)

Quantity

Preferred material

Thickness, if applicable

Indoor or outdoor use

Single-sided or double-sided, if relevant

Colour and finish preferences

Description of the project

Preferred completion date

Pickup, delivery, or installation inquiry

Step 3: Upload Artwork

Allow uploads for relevant artwork files, such as:

PDF

SVG

AI

EPS

PNG

JPG

Set appropriate file-size limits, validate file types, and store files securely.

Step 4: Customer Information

Collect:

Full name

Company name (optional)

Email

Phone number

Project location (optional)

Additional comments

Step 5: Submission

Show a clear confirmation screen and send a confirmation email when email integration is configured.

Generate a unique inquiry number.

All submitted inquiries should appear in the administrator dashboard.

Important: Quote requests must not automatically become accepted orders. A staff member must review costs, production feasibility, and pricing before issuing a final quotation.

14. Overall Experience

The website should feel like a serious, established manufacturing company with an excellent digital customer experience.

Think:

Premium industrial design + modern e-commerce + custom manufacturing services.

I want the site to be visually distinctive, trustworthy, sophisticated, and easy to navigate.

Prioritize the website's public-facing design and working quote-request system first. Then build e-commerce, customer accounts, and the full administrator dashboard in subsequent phases.

Start by creating a polished, mobile-responsive homepage with consistent branding and navigation, followed by the Services, Portfolio, About, Contact, and Custom Quote pages.
